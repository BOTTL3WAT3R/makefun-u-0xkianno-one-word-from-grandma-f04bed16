The word is **artifishal**.

That is the word Grandma texts about AI. Not the word she looked up — the word she heard. Somebody at Sunday lunch said "artificial intelligence", she nodded like she understood, and later she typed it into the family chat exactly the way it sounded to her: *artifishal*. She has spelled it that way ever since, with total confidence, usually right before asking whether the artifishal can fix her printer.

It works as an entry because it is not a joke somebody wrote for her — it is a spelling she would genuinely produce. It names the thing itself (her version of "artificial"), it carries her warm, slightly baffled voice in a single word, and the fish hiding inside it is a bonus she will never notice and would deny if you pointed it out.

The program in this repo writes that one word, and nothing else, to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
